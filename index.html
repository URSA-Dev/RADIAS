Professional Risk Intelligence · Lawyers Professional Liability

The risk you can't independently see, measured for every firm on the bordereau.

Reinsurers assume LPL risk two steps removed from the firms that create it, priced off self-attested applications and cedent summaries. Each named risk is independently assessed from public records: the cedent's detection gap at binding, ceded versus retained on the same measurements, and the exposures correlated across the book. Every figure is traceable to a dated source.
